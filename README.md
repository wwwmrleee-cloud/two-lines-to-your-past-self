Two lines to your past self: a warning about one specific moment, and one sentence on why it mattered. Line one warns against putting off a Tuesday phone call home; line two traces what that delay cost — the last words ever heard.

> When your father calls on Tuesday and asks you to come home, don't say you'll come next weekend.
> Next weekend never comes, and the last thing he ever says to you will be 'take your time.'

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt` (187 characters, 2 lines, UTF-8).
